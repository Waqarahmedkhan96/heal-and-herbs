Pudina is valued for its aromatic, refreshing character and has long been reached for when the stomach feels heavy after food.

How to prepare

One sachet per cup. Pour over just-boiled water, cover so the aroma stays in, and steep for four to five minutes.

Before you use

Peppermint can make heartburn or reflux worse. Not for babies or young children. Ask first if pregnant, breastfeeding or on regular medication.
