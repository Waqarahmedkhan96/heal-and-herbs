In Unani practice

Turmeric, haldi, and ginger, zanjabeel, are both described in Unani texts as warm and dry in temperament. Ginger in particular has long been associated with the stomach.

How to use

Stir one level teaspoon (3 g) into a cup of warm milk or water, or add to soups and curries. Shake the jar before use and close it tightly after.

Before you use

Ask first if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, take blood-thinning or diabetes medication, have gallbladder problems, or have surgery planned.
