طبِ یونانی

A short note on Unani

Unani takes its name from Yunan, the Arabic word for Greece. It grew from the medicine of Hippocrates and Galen and was developed by physicians of the Islamic world, Ibn Sina among them, before taking deep root in South Asia.

At its centre is mizaj, each person’s temperament, and the balance of the body’s four humours. A practitioner looks less at a single complaint and more at the whole pattern: what you eat, how you sleep, how you move and rest.

In Pakistan, Unani practitioners are registered and regulated by the National Council for Tibb.
